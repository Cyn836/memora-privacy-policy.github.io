Memora Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy applies to Memora on iPhone, iPad and Mac, developed by Le Dang Bao (“we”, “us”).

In short: we do not collect, see, sell or share your data. The notifications you save are processed on your devices and stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. There are no accounts to sign up for, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

1. Data we collect

None. We run no servers that receive your data, and the app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. We never know who you are, which notifications you save or how you use the app.

2. How Memora receives your notifications

Memora cannot read your notifications by itself. You set up a Shortcuts automation on your device and choose which apps it applies to. When a notification arrives from one of those apps, the automation passes it to Memora: the app’s name and identifier, the title, the message text and the time, and the app’s icon. Notifications from other apps are never sent to Memora. You can pause saving for an app, or turn the automation off, at any time.

Saved notifications may include messages that other people sent you. Memora treats them the same way as the rest of your data: they stay on your devices and in your iCloud.

3. Where your data is stored

  • On your device. Your saved notifications and app icons, categories, trackers, contacts and profiles, Insight templates and the figures read from your notifications, and your settings are stored inside the app on your device. Sorting into categories, reading amounts and senders, trackers and summaries all run on your device.
  • In your iCloud. If iCloud is turned on, the app syncs your data between your devices through Apple’s CloudKit, in your private iCloud database. This data belongs to your Apple Account and is protected by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. We cannot access it.
  • In Spotlight. If you turn on Search in Spotlight, saved notifications, people and apps are added to your device’s own Spotlight index so you can find them from system search. Hidden notifications and hidden contacts are never added. Turning the option off removes them from the index.

4. Internet connections the app makes

Notification content never leaves your device, except through your own iCloud sync described above. The app makes no connections to our servers. Apart from iCloud sync and the purchase check described below, the only other connection is:

  • Identifying apps via the App Store. To learn an app’s category (for example, whether it is a banking app) and its icon, Memora sends that app’s identifier (its bundle ID) and the region set on your device (for example “US”) to Apple’s public App Store lookup service. Nothing else is sent: no notification content, no names and nothing that identifies you. This option is on by default and can be turned off in Settings › Passcode & Face ID › Identify apps via App Store.

5. Permissions

  • Notifications: used for alerts you create (trackers, summaries at the times you choose, saving limits and connection checks) and for the badge on the app icon. They are scheduled locally on your device. We do not send push messages.
  • Face ID or Touch ID: used only to unlock Memora and your hidden notifications. The check is done by Apple’s system; Memora never sees your biometric data. Your passcode is stored on your device.

6. Purchases

Memora Premium is offered as a subscription or a one-time purchase, processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with Apple whether your purchase is active. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings.

7. Export and sharing

Files you export (CSV, JSON or PDF statements) are created on your device. Where they go afterwards is entirely your choice. The same applies to text you copy and notifications you share. Files you import are read on your device only.

8. Deleting your data

You can delete notifications at any time from within the app, one by one, by selection, or by cleaning up by age, app, sender or words. Deleting the app removes the data on that device. To remove your iCloud copy, go to Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage on iPhone or iPad (System Settings › Apple Account › iCloud on Mac) and delete Memora’s data. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

9. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If the app ever starts collecting any data, we will update this policy before that happens.
